Measuring for a Bra

You'll need a soft tape measure and two minutes. Wear a non-padded bra or measure directly against the skin.

  • Band size: Measure snugly around your ribcage, just under your bust. Round to the nearest even number (37" → 38).
  • Cup size: Measure loosely around the fullest part of your bust. Subtract your band measurement — each inch of difference is one cup letter: 1"=A, 2"=B, 3"=C, 4"=D, 5"=DD.

Example: 39" ribcage rounds to a 40 band. 43" bust is 3 inches more — a C cup. Your size is 40C.
