Accessibility Statement

A store for women over sixty should work beautifully for every woman over sixty. Here is what we do — and how to reach us if something doesn't work for you.

Last updated: January 15, 2026

Our Commitment

SW Lingerie is designed from the ground up for mature shoppers. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and we test our website with customers aged 62 to 84 before every major change.

What We Do

  • Large, adjustable text: use the A / A+ buttons at the top of every page to enlarge all text instantly. Your choice is remembered between visits.
  • High contrast: dark brown text on warm cream exceeds WCAG AA contrast requirements.
  • Big, easy targets: buttons and links are at least 44 pixels tall, with generous spacing.
  • Keyboard navigation: every page can be used without a mouse. Press Tab to move and Enter to select. A "Skip to main content" link appears at the top of each page.
  • Screen-reader support: semantic headings, descriptive labels on every form field, and alternative text on all meaningful images.
  • Reduced motion: if your device is set to reduce motion, animations are switched off automatically.
  • Clear language: we write in plain English, avoid jargon, and explain every step of checkout.
  • Phone ordering: everything on this website can be ordered by phone at 1-800-555-0142 (Mon–Sat, 8am–8pm ET).

Known Limitations

We continually improve. If any part of the site is difficult for you to use — a page that reads poorly in your screen reader, a button that's hard to tap, text that still feels small — we want to know, and we will fix it.
