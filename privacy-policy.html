Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect and why.

Last updated: January 15, 2026 · SW Lingerie, LLC · Portland, Oregon, USA

1. Who We Are

SW Lingerie, LLC ("SW Lingerie," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website swlingerie.shop. We are a family-owned retailer of intimate apparel for mature women, headquartered in Portland, Oregon.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Order information: your name, shipping address, email address, phone number, and the items you purchase.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment providers. We never see or store your full card number — only the last four digits and card brand for your receipt.
  • Communications: emails, phone calls, and contact-form messages you send us, including fit consultations.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you choose to subscribe.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic device and browser information, pages visited, and general location (state-level) to help us keep the website fast and easy to use.
  • Items in your shopping cart, saved on your own device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders, and to provide customer support.
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and — only if you subscribe — our monthly newsletter.
  • To improve our website's readability, speed, and accessibility.
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as tax and accounting requirements.

We never sell your personal information. We do not rent, trade, or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Who We Share Information With

We share only what is necessary, only with service providers who help us operate:

  • Payment processors (to charge your card, PayPal, or Apple Pay).
  • Shipping carriers such as USPS and UPS (your name and delivery address only).
  • Email service providers (to send order updates and newsletters).

Each provider is bound by contract to protect your information and use it only for the service they provide to us.

5. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and browser storage to keep your cart saved, remember your preferred text size, and understand which pages are most helpful. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; the shop will still work, though your cart may not be remembered between visits.

6. How We Protect Your Information

All pages are served over HTTPS with 256-bit TLS encryption. Payment data is handled by PCI-DSS compliant processors. Access to customer records is limited to trained staff who need it to serve you.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access or correct your information by contacting us.
  • Delete your account data and order history, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link at the bottom of any email.
  • State privacy rights: residents of California, Oregon, and other states with privacy laws may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of their data. We honor these requests for all U.S. customers, regardless of state.

To exercise any of these rights, call 1-800-555-0142 or email privacy@swlingerie.shop. We respond within 30 days.

8. Data Retention

We keep order records for seven years for tax and warranty purposes. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. Cart data stored on your device can be cleared at any time through your browser.

9. Children's Privacy

Our store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will post the updated policy on this page with a new "last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify subscribers by email.
