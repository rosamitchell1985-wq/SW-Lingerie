Shipping & Returns

Simple, generous, and clearly explained — because you have better things to do than read fine print.

Last updated: January 15, 2026

Shipping Within the United States

  • Free standard shipping on all orders over $75.
  • Orders under $75 ship for a flat $6.95.
  • We ship to all 50 states, Washington D.C., and U.S. territories via USPS and UPS.
  • Orders leave our Portland, Oregon studio within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days; Alaska, Hawaii, and territories may take 7–10.
  • Every order includes tracking, emailed to you the moment your parcel ships.

Discreet Packaging — Always

Your order arrives in a plain, unbranded box or mailer. The return address reads simply "SWL." Nothing on the outside reveals the contents, and your packing slip is folded inside. What you order is your business.

Our 90-Day Return Promise

You have 90 days from delivery to decide. If something isn't right — the fit, the fabric, the color — we will make it right.

  • Free return shipping: we email you a prepaid USPS return label. Just tape it on the original package and drop it in any mailbox.
  • Full refunds to your original payment method within 5 business days of our receiving your return.
  • Free exchanges for a different size or color — we ship the replacement before your return even arrives.
  • Items should be unworn or gently tried on over undergarments, with liners intact. Washed or worn bras and sleepwear may still be returned within 90 days under our comfort guarantee — we'd rather you love what you keep.

How to Start a Return

  1. Call 1-800-555-0142 (Mon–Sat, 8am–8pm ET) or email returns@swlingerie.shop with your order number.
  2. We'll email your prepaid label the same day.
  3. Package the items, attach the label, and drop them in any mailbox or USPS location.
  4. Your refund or exchange is processed as soon as your return arrives.

No printer? No problem. Call us and we'll mail a printed label to your home.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 14 days and we will ship a replacement immediately at no cost — no return of the damaged item required.

International Orders

At this time we ship within the United States only. We hope to serve international customers in the future.

Questions?

Call 1-800-555-0142 or visit our contact page. A real person will help you.
