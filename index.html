Why we exist

Lingerie that grows with you — not against you.

Most intimate apparel is designed for twenty-somethings and merely resized for everyone else. We started SW Lingerie because our mothers deserved better: bras that close in front, waistbands that don't roll, fabrics that respect thinner, more delicate skin.

Every style is wear-tested by a panel of women aged 62 to 84 before it earns a place in our collection. If they wouldn't wear it, we won't sell it.
